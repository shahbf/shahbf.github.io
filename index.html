Primary research areas

I build tools that let investors and policymakers see climate risk the way markets price it, starting from European physical climate data rather than global proxies.

Nature

Temperature, precipitation, Mediterranean SST, drought (SPEI-12) and NAO, from ERA5 reanalysis.

Uncertainty

Economic policy uncertainty, EU ETS carbon prices and transition-risk signals.

News

European climate media coverage and physical-risk attention indices.

1

Building the European Climate Risk Index (EU-CRI)

A multi-factor index integrating physical climate indicators, news-based indices and policy uncertainty measures to quantify climate risk exposure.

In progress
2

Cross-market climate risk spillovers

How climate shocks travel between European and US markets, across quantiles and frequencies.

Planned
3

Climate risk in asset prices

Whether exposure to the EU-CRI is priced in European equities, and what it means for portfolio optimization.

Planned

About me

Background

I hold an MBA in Finance from Sukkur IBA University and have more than three years of teaching and quantitative research experience across four universities in Pakistan, where I have supervised over 60 student research projects.

Research focus

My doctoral research develops the European Climate Risk Index and studies its implications for portfolio optimization. My work bridges mathematical finance, sustainable finance, systemic risk and empirical asset pricing, using time-series econometrics, GARCH-family models and machine learning.

Vision and ambition

I want to give asset managers and regulators a transparent, regionally grounded measure of climate risk that supports better-informed sustainable investment and climate policy.
